# Home Assistant add-on: Firefox Sync

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield] ![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

Your own Firefox Sync server: Mozilla's syncstorage-rs with its tokenserver, and the
[firefox-sync](https://github.com/porelli/firefox-sync) admin UI in the sidebar, signed in by
Home Assistant. A bundled MariaDB, or the MariaDB add-on. Mozilla's accounts, or your own with
the **Mozilla accounts (self-hosted)** add-on. Home Assistant's backups hold everything, and the
[Firefox Sync integration](https://github.com/porelli/ha-firefox-sync) shows its health.

See the **Documentation** tab.
