# Home Assistant add-on: Mozilla accounts (self-hosted)

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield] ![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

Self-hosted Mozilla accounts for the **Firefox Sync** add-on: sign-up, sign-in and phone
pairing on your own server, from [firefox-sync](https://github.com/porelli/firefox-sync)'s
builds of Mozilla's releases. Needs four host names with HTTPS, an SMTP server, and room: a
5 GB image and 3 to 4 GB of RAM.

See the **Documentation** tab.
