# Home Assistant add-on: AirConnect

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield] ![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

AirPlay for the players that don't have it: philippe44's
[AirConnect](https://github.com/philippe44/AirConnect) finds your UPnP/DLNA players (Sonos,
Heos, Bose SoundTouch, TVs...) and your Chromecasts on the network and turns each of them into
an AirPlay speaker, so that iPhones, iPads, Macs and Apple Music can play to it. Two bridges:
airupnp (UPnP/Sonos) and aircast (Chromecast). You can turn each one on or off. Audio only.

See the **Documentation** tab.
